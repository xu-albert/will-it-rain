Gonna Rain? · Release 2 · design v2

The Live Activity, drawn as the app

Same idea as before — a timeline you glance at — but now it is the app: the exact navy/cyan palette, the bold white hero line, and the signature Next Hour chart (cyan intensity bars, white chance line, dashed "now"). Your lock screen shows a shrunk-down version of the home screen you already know.
